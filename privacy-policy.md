# Privacy Policy for BCA StudyMate

**Last updated: November 2026**

**A JOSH PROJECT** ("we," "our," or "us") operates the **BCA StudyMate** mobile application (the "App"). 

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
**We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII).** 
BCA StudyMate does not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number. 

### 2. Device ID and Progress Tracking
To provide the core functionality of the App (tracking your study progress across different subjects), the App generates a random, anonymous "Device ID." 
- This Device ID is stored locally on your device and is sent to our secure cloud database solely to save your progress (e.g., which units you have marked as completed).
- This ID cannot be used to identify you personally.
- If you uninstall the App, this ID and your progress data may be lost.

### 3. Data Security
Your data is stored securely in a cloud database (TiDB Cloud). Because we do not collect personal information, the risk to your privacy is minimal. However, we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect the anonymous data we store.

### 4. Children's Privacy
Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
