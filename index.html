Privacy Policy for BCA StudyMate

Last updated: November 2026

A JOSH PROJECT ("we," "our," or "us") operates the BCA StudyMate mobile application (the "App").

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII). BCA StudyMate does not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.

2. Device ID and Progress Tracking

To provide the core functionality of the App, the App generates a random, anonymous "Device ID." This is stored locally and sent to our secure cloud database solely to save your progress. This ID cannot be used to identify you personally.

3. Data Security

Your data is stored securely in a cloud database (TiDB Cloud). Because we do not collect personal information, the risk to your privacy is minimal.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
